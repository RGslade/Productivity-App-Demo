# India (IN) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (112)** — `tel:112` — National emergency response for police, fire, and ambulance.
2. **Police (100)** — `tel:100` — Police emergency number in many areas.
3. **Ambulance (108)** — `tel:108` — Medical emergency ambulance support.
4. **National Health Helpline** — https://abha.abdm.gov.in/ — Government digital health and access services.
5. **National Career Service** — https://www.ncs.gov.in/ — Government job search, guidance, and counselling.
6. **Ministry of Labour & Employment** — https://labour.gov.in/ — Labour rights and employment policy information.
7. **EPFO** — https://www.epfindia.gov.in/ — Employee provident fund, retirement and payroll-linked services.
8. **Income Tax e-Filing** — https://www.incometax.gov.in/ — Tax filing and account services.
9. **DigiLocker** — https://www.digilocker.gov.in/ — Secure government document storage and sharing.
10. **National Scholarship Portal** — https://scholarships.gov.in/ — Scholarship discovery and applications.
11. **PMAY Housing** — https://pmaymis.gov.in/ — Affordable housing scheme details and status checks.
12. **MyScheme** — https://www.myscheme.gov.in/ — Discover welfare and support schemes by profile.
13. **Apna Jobs** — https://apna.co/ — Entry-level and early-career job listings.
14. **Naukri** — https://www.naukri.com/ — Major private job portal across India.
15. **AASRA Helpline** — `tel:9152987821` — 24/7 emotional support and crisis listening.
