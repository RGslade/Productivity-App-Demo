# Mexico (MX) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (911)** — `tel:911` — Police, fire, and ambulance emergencies.
2. **Anonymous Crime Reporting (089)** — `tel:089` — Non-emergency anonymous reporting.
3. **Locatel (CDMX support, 24/7)** — `tel:56581111` — Local information and support hotline.
4. **Gobierno de México** — https://www.gob.mx/ — Federal public services and information.
5. **Secretariat of Labor and Social Welfare** — https://www.gob.mx/stps — Labour rights and employment programmes.
6. **IMSS** — http://www.imss.gob.mx/ — Social security and healthcare services.
7. **SAT** — https://www.sat.gob.mx/ — Tax administration and filings.
8. **INFONAVIT** — https://portalmx.infonavit.org.mx/ — Housing credit and worker housing support.
9. **CONDUSEF** — https://www.condusef.gob.mx/ — Financial consumer protection and advice.
10. **IMJUVE** — https://www.gob.mx/imjuve — Youth programmes and opportunities.
11. **Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro** — https://jovenesconstruyendoelfuturo.stps.gob.mx/ — Paid training and early career placements.
12. **Servicio Nacional de Empleo** — https://www.empleo.gob.mx/ — National employment services and vacancies.
13. **OCC Mundial** — https://www.occ.com.mx/ — Private-sector job marketplace.
14. **Computrabajo México** — https://www.computrabajo.com.mx/ — Job search and applications.
15. **Línea de la Vida** — `tel:8009112000` — Mental health, addiction, and crisis support line.
