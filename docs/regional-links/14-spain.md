# Spain (ES) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (112)** — `tel:112` — National emergency line.
2. **National Police (091)** — `tel:091` — Police emergency support.
3. **Medical Emergency (061 in many regions)** — `tel:061` — Urgent medical response.
4. **Administración.gob.es** — https://administracion.gob.es/ — Central government services access.
5. **SEPE** — https://www.sepe.es/ — Employment services and unemployment support.
6. **Seguridad Social** — https://www.seg-social.es/ — Social security services and contributions.
7. **Agencia Tributaria** — https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/ — Tax services and filing.
8. **Portal de Vivienda** — https://www.mivau.gob.es/vivienda — Housing policies and support information.
9. **InfoJobs** — https://www.infojobs.net/ — Major job search platform.
10. **SEPE Formación** — https://www.fundae.es/ — Training and skills development programmes.
11. **UNED** — https://www.uned.es/ — Distance education and qualifications.
12. **Injuve** — https://www.injuve.es/ — Youth opportunities and support resources.
13. **Idealista** — https://www.idealista.com/ — Housing rental and property search.
14. **Fotocasa** — https://www.fotocasa.es/ — Housing listings for rent and sale.
15. **Línea 024** — `tel:024` — Suicide prevention helpline in Spain.
