# Portugal (PT) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (112)** — `tel:112` — National emergency line.
2. **SNS 24** — `tel:808242424` — Non-emergency health guidance and triage.
3. **ePortugal** — https://eportugal.gov.pt/ — Official digital public services portal.
4. **IEFP** — https://www.iefp.pt/ — Employment services, training, and job search.
5. **Portal do Emprego** — https://iefponline.iefp.pt/ — National employment portal.
6. **Autoridade Tributária** — https://www.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/ — Tax account and filing services.
7. **Segurança Social Direta** — https://app.seg-social.pt/ — Social security services and benefits.
8. **Portal da Habitação** — https://www.portaldahabitacao.pt/ — Housing policy and support schemes.
9. **DGES** — https://www.dges.gov.pt/ — Higher education admissions and guidance.
10. **Casa para Arrendar** — https://www.casaparaarrendar.pt/ — Rental housing search.
11. **Idealista Portugal** — https://www.idealista.pt/ — Housing listings and rentals.
12. **Net-Empregos** — https://www.net-empregos.com/ — Private job listings.
13. **SAPO Emprego** — https://emprego.sapo.pt/ — Career opportunities and applications.
14. **SNS** — https://www.sns.gov.pt/ — National health service information.
15. **SOS Voz Amiga** — `tel:213544545` — Emotional support helpline.
