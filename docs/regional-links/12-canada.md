# Canada (CA) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (911)** — `tel:911` — Police, fire, and ambulance emergencies.
2. **Wellness Together Canada** — https://www.wellnesstogether.ca/ — Mental health and substance use support.
3. **Canada.ca** — https://www.canada.ca/ — Federal government services and guidance.
4. **Service Canada** — https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/corporate/portfolio/service-canada.html — Employment insurance and social services access.
5. **Job Bank** — https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/ — National job board and labour market information.
6. **CRA** — https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html — Tax filing and benefits administration.
7. **Canada Student Loans and Grants** — https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/student-aid.html — Student financial support.
8. **CMHC** — https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/ — Housing guidance and affordability resources.
9. **211 Canada** — https://211.ca/ — Community and non-emergency social support directory.
10. **Kids Help Phone** — `tel:18006686868` — 24/7 counselling for young people.
11. **Health Canada** — https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada.html — National health and safety information.
12. **Immigration and Citizenship** — https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship.html — Immigration and settlement services.
13. **Indeed Canada** — https://ca.indeed.com/ — Private job opportunities across Canada.
14. **LinkedIn Jobs Canada** — https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/ — Professional networking and careers.
15. **Tenant Rights (CLEO resource)** — https://www.cleo.on.ca/en/publications/tenantsaccess/all — Plain-language tenant rights and housing help.
