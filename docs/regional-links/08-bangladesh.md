# Bangladesh (BD) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **National Emergency Service (999)** — `tel:999` — Police, fire, and ambulance response.
2. **Police Helpline (999)** — `tel:999` — Police support through the national emergency system.
3. **Women & Child Helpline (109)** — `tel:109` — Protection and crisis support.
4. **National Portal Bangladesh** — https://bangladesh.gov.bd/ — Government services and ministry access.
5. **Ministry of Labour and Employment** — https://mole.gov.bd/ — Labour regulation and workplace rights.
6. **NID Services** — https://services.nidw.gov.bd/ — National ID services and updates.
7. **National Board of Revenue** — https://nbr.gov.bd/ — Tax information and e-services.
8. **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare** — https://mohfw.gov.bd/ — Public healthcare policy and services.
9. **Department of Youth Development** — https://dyd.gov.bd/ — Skills and youth programme opportunities.
10. **Bangladesh Bureau of Educational Information** — http://www.banbeis.gov.bd/ — Education data and policy resources.
11. **BDJobs** — https://www.bdjobs.com/ — Large employment marketplace.
12. **Chakri.com** — https://www.chakri.com/ — Job listings and recruitment support.
13. **Bproperty** — https://www.bproperty.com/ — Property search for rent and purchase.
14. **Moner Jotno Mobile E-Clinic** — https://www.monerjotno.com/ — Mental health support services.
15. **Ain o Salish Kendra** — https://www.askbd.org/ — Legal aid and rights support.
