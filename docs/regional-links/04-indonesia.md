# Indonesia (ID) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (112)** — `tel:112` — General emergency contact in supported regions.
2. **Police (110)** — `tel:110` — Police emergency number.
3. **Ambulance (118/119)** — `tel:118` — Medical emergency ambulance support.
4. **Government Portal** — https://www.indonesia.go.id/ — Official national information and service links.
5. **Ministry of Manpower** — https://kemnaker.go.id/ — Employment rights, wage, and labour resources.
6. **BPJS Kesehatan** — https://www.bpjs-kesehatan.go.id/ — National health insurance services.
7. **Directorate General of Taxes** — https://www.pajak.go.id/ — Tax services and e-filing information.
8. **Ministry of Public Works and Housing** — https://pu.go.id/ — Housing and infrastructure policy resources.
9. **Prakerja** — https://www.prakerja.go.id/ — Upskilling support and workforce programmes.
10. **Satu Data Indonesia** — https://data.go.id/ — Public data and policy information platform.
11. **Kartu Indonesia Pintar Kuliah** — https://kip-kuliah.kemdikbud.go.id/ — Higher education funding support.
12. **JobStreet Indonesia** — https://www.jobstreet.co.id/ — Regional job search platform.
13. **Karir.com** — https://www.karir.com/ — Indonesian job listings and employer profiles.
14. **Glints Indonesia** — https://glints.com/id — Graduate and early-career opportunities.
15. **Into The Light Indonesia** — https://www.intothelightid.org/ — Youth mental health advocacy and support resources.
