# Nigeria (NG) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (112)** — `tel:112` — National emergency line.
2. **Police (199/112 where available)** — `tel:199` — Emergency policing support in supported zones.
3. **Lagos Emergency (767/112)** — `tel:767` — Regional rapid response where available.
4. **Nigeria Government Portal** — https://www.nigeria.gov.ng/ — Official public-service access point.
5. **Federal Ministry of Labour & Employment** — https://labour.gov.ng/ — Employment policy and labour rights.
6. **National Health Insurance Authority** — https://www.nhia.gov.ng/ — Health coverage and enrolment details.
7. **Federal Inland Revenue Service** — https://www.firs.gov.ng/ — Tax information and services.
8. **National Identity Management Commission** — https://nimc.gov.ng/ — National ID registration and lookup guidance.
9. **National Social Safety Nets** — https://nassp.gov.ng/ — Social protection programmes.
10. **National Housing Programme** — https://fmhud.gov.ng/ — Housing and urban development resources.
11. **JAMB** — https://www.jamb.gov.ng/ — Tertiary admissions and exam information.
12. **MyJobMag** — https://www.myjobmag.com/ — Job listings and career content.
13. **Jobberman** — https://www.jobberman.com/ — Employment search and career tools.
14. **Hotline for Domestic Violence (Lagos DSVA)** — `tel:08000333333` — Crisis and safeguarding support.
15. **Mentally Aware Nigeria Initiative** — https://mentallyaware.org/ — Mental health awareness and support resources.
