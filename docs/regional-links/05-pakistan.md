# Pakistan (PK) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (15)** — `tel:15` — Police emergency support.
2. **Rescue Service (1122)** — `tel:1122` — Fire, rescue, and ambulance emergency services in many provinces.
3. **Edhi Ambulance (115)** — `tel:115` — Nationwide ambulance and welfare service.
4. **Pakistan Government Portal** — https://www.pakistan.gov.pk/ — National services and ministry links.
5. **Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis & HRD** — https://ophrd.gov.pk/ — Labour and skills development policy.
6. **National Job Portal** — https://njp.gov.pk/ — Public-sector job opportunities.
7. **Federal Board of Revenue** — https://www.fbr.gov.pk/ — Tax and registration services.
8. **Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal** — https://www.pbm.gov.pk/ — Public welfare and financial support schemes.
9. **Ehsaas Programme** — https://www.pass.gov.pk/ — Poverty alleviation and social support services.
10. **Higher Education Commission** — https://www.hec.gov.pk/ — Scholarships and higher education resources.
11. **NAVTTC** — https://navttc.gov.pk/ — Technical and vocational training support.
12. **Rozee.pk** — https://www.rozee.pk/ — Major private job platform.
13. **Mustakbil** — https://www.mustakbil.com/ — Job discovery across sectors.
14. **Zameen** — https://www.zameen.com/ — Housing rentals and property search.
15. **Umang Pakistan Helpline** — `tel:03117778626` — Mental health and emotional support helpline.
