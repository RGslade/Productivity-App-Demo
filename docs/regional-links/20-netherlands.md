# Netherlands (NL) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (112)** — `tel:112` — Police, fire, and ambulance emergencies.
2. **Police (non-emergency 0900-8844)** — `tel:09008844` — Non-urgent police contact.
3. **113 Suicide Prevention** — `tel:113` — Crisis and suicide prevention support.
4. **Rijksoverheid** — https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/ — Central government information portal.
5. **Werk.nl (UWV)** — https://www.werk.nl/ — Jobs, unemployment, and benefits support.
6. **Belastingdienst** — https://www.belastingdienst.nl/ — Tax services and filings.
7. **DigiD** — https://www.digid.nl/ — Digital identity for government services.
8. **Zorgverzekeringlijn** — https://www.zorgverzekeringlijn.nl/ — Health insurance guidance.
9. **DUO** — https://www.duo.nl/ — Student finance and education support.
10. **Kamernet** — https://kamernet.nl/ — Student rooms and rental housing.
11. **Pararius** — https://www.pararius.com/ — Rental housing listings.
12. **Indeed Netherlands** — https://nl.indeed.com/ — Job opportunities and employer search.
13. **LinkedIn Jobs Netherlands** — https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/ — Professional roles and networking.
14. **Juridisch Loket** — https://www.juridischloket.nl/ — Free legal information and referrals.
15. **Nationale Ombudsman** — https://www.nationaleombudsman.nl/ — Complaints about public services.
