2. **Police (17)** — `tel:17` — Police emergencies.
3. **Medical Emergency (15 - SAMU)** — `tel:15` — Urgent medical assistance.
4. **Service-Public.fr** — https://www.service-public.fr/ — Official French public services portal.
5. **France Travail** — https://www.francetravail.fr/ — National employment services and job search.
6. **CAF** — https://www.caf.fr/ — Family and housing allowances.
7. **Ameli** — https://www.ameli.fr/ — Public health insurance services.
8. **Impots.gouv.fr** — https://www.impots.gouv.fr/ — Tax administration and filing.
9. **ANIL** — https://www.anil.org/ — Housing and tenancy advice.
10. **Mon Compte Formation** — https://www.moncompteformation.gouv.fr/ — Training and upskilling support.
11. **Parcoursup** — https://www.parcoursup.fr/ — Higher education application platform.
12. **CIDJ** — https://www.cidj.com/ — Youth guidance on jobs, studies, and mobility.
13. **Apec** — https://www.apec.fr/ — Career support and job offers.
14. **Doctolib** — https://www.doctolib.fr/ — Healthcare booking and services access.
15. **SOS Amitié** — `tel:0969394000` — Emotional support helpline.
