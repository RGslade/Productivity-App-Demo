# China (CN) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (110)** — `tel:110` — Police emergency support.
2. **Medical Emergency (120)** — `tel:120` — Ambulance and urgent medical dispatch.
3. **Fire Services (119)** — `tel:119` — Fire emergency number.
4. **Government Service Platform** — https://www.gov.cn/ — National government information and online services.
5. **Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security** — http://www.mohrss.gov.cn/ — Jobs, labour protections, and social security.
6. **National Healthcare Security Administration** — http://www.nhsa.gov.cn/ — Public health insurance policy and services.
7. **State Taxation Administration** — https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/ — Tax policy and online tax services.
8. **Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development** — https://www.mohurd.gov.cn/ — Housing rules and urban housing policy.
9. **12333 Human Resources Hotline** — `tel:12333` — Social security and employment enquiries.
10. **12348 Legal Aid Hotline** — `tel:12348` — Public legal advice and legal aid referrals.
11. **China Youth Development Foundation** — https://www.cydf.org.cn/ — Youth education and social support initiatives.
12. **China Scholarship Council** — https://www.csc.edu.cn/ — Scholarship and exchange programme information.
13. **Zhaopin** — https://www.zhaopin.com/ — National job platform for graduates and professionals.
14. **51job** — https://www.51job.com/ — Private job listings and employer search.
15. **Liepin** — https://www.liepin.com/ — Career opportunities and recruiter network.
