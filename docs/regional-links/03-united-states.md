# United States (US) - Young Adult Essential Links

1. **Emergency Services (911)** — `tel:911` — Police, fire, and ambulance emergencies.
2. **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — `tel:988` — 24/7 mental health crisis support.
3. **211 Community Help** — `tel:211` — Local referrals for housing, food, and social support.
4. **USA.gov** — https://www.usa.gov/ — Official federal government services directory.
5. **Benefits.gov** — https://www.benefits.gov/ — Check eligibility for public support programs.
6. **Department of Labor** — https://www.dol.gov/ — Worker rights, wages, and labour resources.
7. **CareerOneStop** — https://www.careeronestop.org/ — Job search tools and career planning resources.
8. **IRS** — https://www.irs.gov/ — Federal tax filing and account services.
9. **HealthCare.gov** — https://www.healthcare.gov/ — Health insurance marketplace.
10. **HUD Rental Assistance** — https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance — Housing support and tenant resources.
11. **SAMHSA Helpline** — `tel:18006624357` — Confidential treatment referral and information.
12. **USAJOBS** — https://www.usajobs.gov/ — Federal government job opportunities.
13. **Indeed** — https://www.indeed.com/ — Broad job listings and applications.
14. **LinkedIn Jobs** — https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/ — Professional job search and networking.
15. **Federal Student Aid** — https://studentaid.gov/ — Grants, loans, and repayment support.
